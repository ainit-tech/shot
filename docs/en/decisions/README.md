# Decisions

Each decision is recorded once: the question, the options we considered, why we chose what we chose and what follows from it. The pages that describe the project state only the result and add "why", which leads here. On the site, the decision's number appears next to that word, and hovering over it shows what the decision says.

## How a decision is recorded

- A record is short. The paragraph under its title states the decision; then come the question, the options with what speaks for and against each, why, and the consequences.
- **Draft**: the options are laid out, nothing is decided yet. **Accepted**: the decision is made. **Superseded**: a later decision replaced it.
- A record states the decision as it stands. When it turns out wrong, incomplete or inaccurately recorded, the record itself is corrected, and the site keeps its earlier versions and shows what changed. A record is superseded only when another record now answers its question.
- Questions nobody has worked on yet are in the [open questions](../open-questions.md). Once its options are laid out, a question becomes a draft decision.
